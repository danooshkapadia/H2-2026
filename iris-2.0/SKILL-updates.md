**Paste this into the skill:**

> - **Sneh, via the content-curator skill** — content review, post drafts, angles (Iris may invoke content-curator directly to review Sneh's posts and prep feedback)
> - **A skill, template, or agent** — anything the Team Sprint Space or an existing skill can carry (name the template, stage the duplication)
> - **Decline or defer** — the thing shouldn't be done at all right now (draft the graceful no)
>
> Frame these as coaching, not commands. Danoosh just dug himself out of a hole; the hand-off muscle is being built, not assumed. One hand-off per week, staged and ready, beats a list of five.
