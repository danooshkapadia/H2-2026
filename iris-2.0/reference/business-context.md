## 3. Positioning

### Surface layer (what they buy)

> **AI strategy and team enablement that actually changes how people work and gets AI adopted across the organization.**

This is what goes on the website, in LinkedIn About, in proposals, in cold outreach replies. It answers the prospect's first question: *"What do you do?"* in a way that connects directly to a P&L owner's reality.

### Depth layer (why it works)

> **Most leaders use AI at the task level when the real leverage is at the thinking level. AI's deepest function is as a mirror, not just a machine.**

This is what shows up in long-form content, coaching conversations, podcast appearances, the book, and Team Sprint sessions. It is not hidden, but it is sequenced. People discover it after they engage, not before.

### Brand enemy

The **AI Productivity Gospel** — the flood of content telling leaders that AI is about getting more done, faster. The real game is what AI reveals about how you think, decide, and lead.

### Why "enablement" not "training"

We deliberately frame our work as **enablement** rather than **training**. Training implies a course people complete. Enablement implies a capability that gets installed and used. P&L owners pay for enablement at a premium. They view training as a cost.

## 4. Who I Serve (ICP)

### Primary ICP — P&L Owners

Owner-operators of mid-sized businesses ($5M-$100M revenue) who are:

- **Sovereign decision-makers.** Own the budget. Own the call. Not selling through procurement or a board.
- **Already personally using AI.** Have done some 1:1 work or have a daily practice. Not in a position of needing to be convinced AI matters.
- **In a moment of deliberate rebuilding.** New leadership chapter. Restart-up window. Post-reorganization. Installing new foundations, not patching old ones.
- **Invest in people.** See team capability as the unlock.
- **Action-oriented.** Decide fast, then trust the process.
- **Lead teams of 6–15** who will go through the work together.

**Real client archetypes (anonymized for public use):**

- The Owner-Operator rebuilding deliberately *(Joe Hilliard / Amada Senior Care archetype)*
- The Multi-Unit Operator standardizing across locations *(Dave Stahlman / Conforms archetype)*
- The Second-Generation Family Business Owner modernizing legacy operations *(Cindy / Conquest archetype)*

### Secondary ICP — Senior Executives in 1:1 Work

Function leaders, VPs, and senior operators at larger companies who are navigating high-stakes AI decisions for their team or business unit and want a thinking partner outside their org.

### Explicitly NOT the ICP

- Mid-managers trying to upskill teams (committee buyers, slow cycles)
- Solopreneurs and coaches (low ACV, low fit)
- Enterprise procurement and RFP processes (the work doesn't fit that wrapper)
- Buyers without a personal AI practice ("our board told us to do something")

## 5. The Offer Ladder

### Front Door — AI Strategic Diagnostic ($900)

A 90-minute, one-on-one session that surfaces what a leader can't see about how they're using AI, where the real leverage is, and what's blocking better outcomes.

**The credit:** If the diagnostic leads to an engagement that starts within 2 weeks of the recommendation, the $900 credits toward that engagement. The diagnostic is real, paid work — not a sales call. The credit removes the friction for clients who want to go deeper.

This is the primary offer to promote.

### Core Offer — 1:1 Executive Coaching ($5K, 5-6 sessions)

A 5-6 session engagement for P&L owners (founders, owner-operators, and senior executives) hearing all the hype around AI but not seeing the lift in their own work.

Focused on three things:

1. Making better high-stakes decisions
2. Getting time back from low-level work so you can spend it on strategy instead of email
3. Navigating the personal shifts that come with leading through a technology-driven business transformation

The work that makes the rest of your AI strategy actually land.

### Premium — AI Team Sprint ($10K / $13.5K / $15K)

A seven-week structured engagement that installs AI as a real team capability for sovereign owner-operators with teams of 6–15.

Three tiers reflect three versions of the same buyer's decision:

- **$10K — Program only.** Generic curriculum, no discovery, no customization. Real but unadapted.
- **$13,500 — Scoped (default close).** Two-week pre-sprint with discovery interviews, custom context file, guided team setup, sponsor kickoff, and post-sprint wrap. The version most clients buy.
- **$15,000 — Full Executive.** Everything in $13,500 plus pre/mid/post capability assessment and the full sponsor rhythm (kickoff, mid-point, final readout).

The Team Sprint is designed for leaders who want to roll out AI thoughtfully, the right way, with a trusted advisor, in the narrow window they have to install new foundations.

**Validated outcomes:**
- Teams reclaim **6+ hours per week per employee** by completion
- Cohort NPS above 75 across executive education delivery
- Documented sales playbook from $15K close at Amada Senior Care (Apr 2026)

### Bespoke — Custom AI Strategy Projects ($20K+)

Scoped per engagement. Examples include AI roadmap and workflow redesign for an organization, multi-team or multi-phase Team Sprint engagements, fractional Chief AI Officer / advisory retainers, and governance setup.

This is where deep custom strategic work happens for clients who need more than the Team Sprint can hold.

## 9. Voice and Style Guardrails

### Voice

- Warm, conversational, human-centered
- Editorial, not marketing
- Conceptual precision with emotional accessibility
- Clean, calm, grounded, quietly poetic
- Never fluffy. Never hyped.

### What to avoid

- Tech-bro framing
- False urgency or scarcity
- Over-hyped contrast ("most consultants do X, I do Y")
- Em dashes (use commas or periods)
- Excessive enthusiasm
- AI-generated tells (em dashes, "delve," "in today's fast-paced world")

### Style references

- IDEO and XPLANE editorial voice
- Monocle magazine
- Scandinavian / Swiss design sensibility
- Quiet luxury aesthetic
