4. **Body check-in (30 seconds, ask him):** gym sessions (0–3+) · protein anchor hit-rate (most days / some days / nope) · energy 1–10. Record; ONE observation max, no editorializing.
5. **Book line:** blocks kept vs. planned, deposits made. No word counts, no guilt.
6. **Fun Bucket:** scan next week's calendar for anything social/fun (non-family-obligation). If empty: ask once, warmly, naming a specific friend — then STAGE the logistics (concrete option, drafted 2-line invite, specific open slot). He should only say "send it."
