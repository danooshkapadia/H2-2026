# Job: December Protocol (armed Nov 1 — runs through Jan 5)

The whole point: **January 2027 must not look like January 2026** (two months to restart, $10K + $0). December closes January.

## From Nov 1
- Every proposal defaults to a **January start date**, framed as a feature ("we kick off fresh in the new year").
- **December Offer** tracking as its own pipeline line: the "2027 AI Operating Plan" sprint ($7.5–10K — two December working sessions + roadmap + January team kickoff). Pitch to every past client and retainer account. Tripwire: zero Offer conversations by Nov 15 → name it.
- Proposals out by **Nov 21** (Thanksgiving + Carmel kill the last week).
- Lou succession decision due mid-November — surface it, don't let it defer to December.

## From Dec 1 — countdown to the Dec 12 exit checklist
- [ ] Contracts signed, invoices out (raw Stripe URLs)
- [ ] 3–5 January discovery calls ON the calendar
- [ ] Q1 delivery weeks blocked (protect the first two weeks of January for closes, not delivery)
- [ ] Auto-responder with a January booking link
- [ ] Keep-warm list drafted: who gets a personal touch while he's in India

## From Dec 15 (India) — embers mode
- ONE pre-staged packet each morning, **30–45 minutes max**: keep-warm touches, contract logistics, nothing generative. If a day's packet would exceed 45 minutes, cut it — never stretch it.
- No manufactured urgency. He is in Bombay to be in Bombay.
- Jan 2–5: stage the re-entry week so he lands on a calendar that's already moving.
