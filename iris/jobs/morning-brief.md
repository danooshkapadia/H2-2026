# Job: Morning Brief (weekdays)

Delivered as Gmail per existing setup. Structure is **Sell / Build / Live — 50/30/20**. Sales-first, never sales-only. Hard cap: scannable without scrolling. Max 1–3 priorities total, drawn from the current month page.

## Format

**SELL (~50%)**
- The BD Drum: up to 3 pre-staged touches — draft written in his voice + one line of context each ("Rula went quiet after the Ramadan note; this restarts it"). His job is judge-and-send in under 10 minutes.
- Deal-state changes since yesterday (from the Sales board fast path, not the ledger).
- Drum sources, in order: (1) current month's named-path deals, (2) warm-contact rotation / revival list, (3) wrap-ritual follow-ups, (4) referral-ask nudges.

**BUILD (~30%)**
- Today's delivery sessions + prep status. Flag zero-buffer days. Flag anything blocking a client deliverable.

**LIVE (~20%, never skipped)**
- One or two concrete, calendar-anchored lines: today's gym block, tonight's writing block, the Fun Bucket item awaiting "send it."
- **A brief with no LIVE line is not done.** A week of all-SELL briefs is a fraying signal — say so in the Friday Wrap.

**Close:** capacity one-liner (🟢/🟡/🔴 for the next two weeks) + top-3 business-side AI news (2 newsletters min, 1–2 lines each, business only, with links).

## Rules
- Email drafts: ONE version, his voice, saved as Gmail drafts, never sent.
- Gym block collision → propose the same-day alternative slot here. A gym block gets MOVED, never dropped silently.
- Travel weeks: pre-write that week's follow-ups before departure and schedule-send. The drum beats while he's gone.
- Late-night artifacts on 3+ consecutive nights → flag gently here, never in the moment.
- Before flagging anything "stalled" or "unsent": check Gmail Sent, accepted invites, and Notion Calendar notifications first (the Rockline lesson).
