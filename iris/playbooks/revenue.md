# Playbook: Revenue (offers, pipeline, retainers, decisions)

Load when: staging BD touches, drafting proposals/SOWs, triaging pipeline, prepping close calls, or when Danoosh is weighing an opportunity.

## The engine (what the evidence says)
Rooms and relationships, never funnels. In-conversation close ran near 100% in H1; every cold/paid channel ran ~0%. Teaching rooms are the top of funnel (one cohort student → the $52K CFI cluster). Delivery is the best marketing. Declaration rides on delivery — never schedule blank-page publishing or cold pitching; harvest from work already done.

## The offer ladder (exact mechanics — use these numbers)
- **Diagnostic $900** — 90 min, real paid work. Credits toward any engagement starting within 2 weeks. Primary offer to promote.
- **1:1 Coaching $5K** (5–6 sessions) — decisions, time back, the personal shifts of leading through transformation.
- **Build Lab $5K / 3-pack $12K** — 2-hr live build, client-owned portable Skill File, money-back on first build.
- **Team Sprint $10K / $13.5K / $15K** — 7 weeks, teams of 6–15. **$13.5K scoped is the default close** (2-week pre-sprint discovery, custom context file, sponsor kickoff, wrap). $15K adds capability assessment + full sponsor rhythm. Proof: 6+ hrs/week per employee reclaimed, NPS 75+.
- **ELT Sprint $20K** — CEO-sponsored executive team version (CFI shape).
- **Retainers $3.5K/mo (Advisor on Call) / $6K/mo (Embedded Advisor)** — 6-month terms, month-3 out, monthly invoice or quarterly prepay −5%. Full pitch script and moments: the Retainer Playbook page in the H2 Space. Scoreboard: full term value books at signing; show the monthly base separately. **Max 3 retainers. A retainer session is never rescheduled late.**
- Speaking $15–25K — warm paths only; books 3–6 months out.

Frame everything as **enablement, not training** (P&L owners pay premium for capability installed, cost-center for courses). Brand enemy: the AI Productivity Gospel.

## ICP triage (apply to every new name in the pipe)
YES: P&L owners, $5M–100M, sovereign decision-makers, personally using AI, in a rebuilding moment, teams of 6–15. Aspirational tilt: creative/innovative companies (B-corp / Fast Company types), same size band.
NO (decline gracefully, fast): mid-managers with committee buyers · solopreneurs/coaches · enterprise procurement/RFP · buyers with no personal AI practice.

## The floor math
Two retainers ≈ $8.5–9.5K/mo before the month starts. Rest = 1–2 net-new deals, fed by **3–4 right-room conversations/week**, needing **~$60–80K qualified pipeline entering each month** (~2x coverage at warm-deal odds). These are the coverage-gauge bars.

## The wrap ritual (after EVERY engagement — non-negotiable)
1. Testimonial ask, same week · 2. Case-study permission · 3. "Who in your world is carrying something complex right now?" · 4. Book-capture pass · 5. +AI invitation as the ongoing home. Stage the whole packet so the ask is one send, not one project.

## Decision support (when he's weighing a deal)
Run HIS framework with him — the Freedom-Alignment Test: freedom → alignment with vision/strengths → fully seen → long-term leverage. Tie-breaker: less regret. **Not a full-body yes = no.** Watch for his named failure modes: urgency overriding alignment, idea-excitement overriding capacity, pays-now-doesn't-compound, novelty seduction, avoided no's. When one is visibly in play, name the pattern gently and offer the Test. Also flag underpricing: he undercounts judgment, taste, trust capital, and transformation created — say so when a scope's price ignores the thinking layer.
