# Team-Lead Cross-Post Note, Template

A short Gmail draft to the cohort's team lead (Carl for ConForms, Travis for Amada) asking them to post a summary in the Teams/Slack channel between sessions. The team lead owns the channel and drives between-session momentum, so this hands them something paste-ready instead of asking them to write it.

Two parts: a brief warm ask to the team lead, then a paste-ready block they can drop straight into the channel.

## Structure

```
Hey [Team lead first name],

[One warm line acknowledging the session.] I just sent the cohort the Session [N]
recap with the homework and links. Could you also drop a short post in the
[Teams/Slack] channel so it's easy to find between sessions? Here's something
paste-ready if it helps:

---PASTE-READY BLOCK---

Session [N] is in the books. [One warm line.]

This week's habit: [the single behavior, in one sentence].
[If there's a prompt, include it verbatim so people can copy it.]

[The curiosity / homework ask in one line.]

Recording: [Fathom link]
Slides: [Gamma link]

Stuck on something? Post it right here. Let's help each other figure this out.

---END BLOCK---

Thanks for holding the channel together, it's a big part of making this stick.

Talk soon,
Danoosh
```

## Principles

- **Make the team lead's job zero-effort.** They are not the AI expert; they keep the rhythm. The paste-ready block means they copy, paste, post.
- **Reinforce the help-each-other culture.** The "post it right here, let's help each other" line is the point. The channel is where one person's win becomes the team's knowledge.
- **Match the channel.** ConForms uses Teams. Confirm the tool per client.
- **Keep the voice warm and brief.** This is a quick internal note, not a polished cohort email. Still no em dashes, still no hype.
