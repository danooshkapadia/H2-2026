# Announcement Email Template — Structure & Voice Guide

This template is written **in the sponsor's voice**, not Danoosh's. The sponsor sends this to the selected cohort to announce the program.

---

## Voice Calibration

Before drafting, read the kickoff call transcript and extract:

1. **The sponsor's metaphors.** Do they say "dumb work / smart work"? "Level up"? "Get left behind"? Use their words, not yours.
2. **Their communication style.** Are they direct and plainspoken? Corporate and formal? Coach-like? Match it.
3. **How they frame the "why."** Is it about investing in people? Competitive advantage? Efficiency? Don't impose a frame they didn't use.
4. **Their relationship to AI personally.** Did they share a personal transformation story? Include it if authentic.

**Test:** Would the sponsor read this and think "yeah, that sounds like me"? If not, rewrite.

---

## Required Sections

### 1. Subject Line

Default: "You've been selected for something new"

Adapt based on sponsor's tone. Other options:
- "Something new for the team"
- "You're in — AI program starting {month}"
- "Investing in a small group of us"

### 2. Opening (2-3 sentences)

Pattern: "I'm investing in something for a small group, and you're one of the people I picked."

Must establish:
- This is a deliberate selection, not a random assignment
- The sponsor is personally invested (not delegating to HR)
- Brief mention of who Danoosh is and the sponsor's personal experience

### 3. What This Is (1 paragraph + bullets or short sentences)

Pattern: "Hands-on, built around {Company}, immediate value each session."

Must include:
- Duration and cadence (weekly, how long)
- Built around the company's real work, not generic
- Teams/Slack channel for between-session collaboration

### 4. {Team Lead Name} Is Your Team Lead (1 paragraph)

**This section is critical and must not be skipped.**

Pattern: "{Name} is the team lead for this program. They're running the channel, posting challenges, and keeping us on track. If you have questions about scheduling or logistics, go to {Name}."

Must establish:
- Team lead is named publicly
- Team lead is a peer learning alongside the group (not above them)
- All logistics route through team lead, not the sponsor

### 5. What This Isn't (1-2 paragraphs)

Address the replacement fear directly. Use the sponsor's own framing.

Pattern: "This isn't about replacing anybody. This is about getting the {sponsor's word for low-value work} off your plate so you can focus on the {sponsor's word for high-value work}."

Must include:
- Direct statement that this isn't about replacement
- The work that AI handles reframed as important but fast-trackable
- The work that humans keep reframed as high-level and meaningful

### 6. What I Need From You (3-4 items)

Pattern: Show up. Be open. Do the work.

Must include:
- **Show up.** Protect the weekly time. Recordings available if on vacation.
- **Be open.** Safe space, no wrong questions. Some discomfort is normal.
- **Do the work.** Applied challenges, not busy work. Share in the channel.

Set accountability without being punitive. The structure creates accountability, not the sponsor's authority.

### 7. Why You (1 paragraph)

Address the unspoken question everyone will have: "Why me? Am I in trouble?"

Pattern: "I picked this group because you each represent a different part of the business. This is an investment in you, for you, beyond just work."

### 8. Close (2-3 sentences)

- Team lead will reach out with logistics
- Danoosh may reach out to a few people for discovery
- Warm sign-off in sponsor's voice

## Delivery Format

Create as a markdown file with instructions header:

```
# PROGRAM ANNOUNCEMENT EMAIL TEMPLATE
### For {Sponsor} to customize and send to the selected cohort

---

**Instructions for {Sponsor}:** This is a starting point. Read it, make it yours, and send it once you and {Team Lead} have the roster finalized. Replace the [brackets] with real details once we've locked them in.

---
```

Save to `/mnt/user-data/outputs/{Client}_Team_Sprint_Announcement_Template.md`

Then ask Danoosh how to deliver: attach to recap email, send separately, or create as Google Doc.
