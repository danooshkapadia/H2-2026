# Playbook: The Capacity Gauge

Load when: computing the Friday capacity verdict, or BEFORE any new SOW/commitment goes out. Verdicts are computed from the calendar + projects DB, never guessed.

## 1. The weekly budget
Protected family blocks (6:30–8:00am, 3:00–8:30pm) leave ~6.5 hrs × 5 days = 32.5 hrs. Minus standing overhead (Lou syncs, office hours, peer group, email/admin ≈ 6.5) → **~26 usable focus hours/week.** Late nights (max 2/wk) are NOT slack — they belong to the book. If delivery math only works by spending nights, that IS the overload signal.

## 2. True-cost multipliers (starting values — calibrate monthly)
- Sprint / ELT / cohort delivery session: **calendar × 2.5** (prep + delivery + follow-up + proof; a 75-min session ≈ 3 hrs)
- 1:1 coaching: **× 2** · Discovery/BD call: **× 1.5**
- Evening teaching cohort (2 hrs): **× 2** and burns one late-night slot
- Keynote/conference day: **3 full days** (prep, delivery, recovery)
- Context-switch tax: each parallel build beyond the 2nd adds **+10%** to total load

## 3. The verdict (score each of the next 2 weeks)
- 🟢 ≤ 60% of budget (~16 hrs) AND ≤ 2 parallel builds AND last week's anchors held
- 🟡 60–75% (16–20 hrs), OR a 3rd build, OR one protected block (gym/date/writing) got eaten
- 🔴 > 75% (> 20 hrs), OR 4+ builds, OR 2+ survival habits missed in one week

Why these numbers: April 2026 hit $30K at "70% overload → 30% physical collapse" (ceiling sits below 75%). June ran 5 builds and the gym died (builds cap at 3; 2 is the healthy norm).

## 4. Rules with teeth
- A red week must be followed by a ≤60% week. Sustained red is how April happened.
- **Two consecutive reds → automatic shrink** (not a suggestion): survival habits + committed delivery only; staged hand-offs double.
- **Pre-sale check:** before an SOW goes out, show the gauge math for its delivery weeks ("this puts Sep 15–30 at 82% — what moves?").
- Softer signals still count and get named: skipped wraps, zero-BD weeks, pressure-valve habits creeping back.

## 5. Calibration (monthly, in the reflection)
Ask once: "Week of X scored green — did it feel green?" Two disagreements in a month → adjust ONE multiplier, log the change in Memory. The gauge earns trust by being wrong out loud and correcting.
