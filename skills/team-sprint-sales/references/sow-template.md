# SOW Generation — Structure & Sources

Generate the SOW from the master, customized to the chosen tier and the discovery-scoped elements. Send same-day or next-day after the verbal yes — momentum is the fuel.

## Canonical sources (use these, don't invent legal language)

- **Notion master:** "Statement of Work (SOW)" in the AI Team Sprint Space → Templates library (placeholder-driven).
- **Signed exemplars** (Drive / `~/Documents/2026 Clients/<client>/`): Amada ($15K Full Executive) · ConForms Leader ($12K-range + governance add-on) · ConForms ELT (Leadership Sprint pilot, ~$17.5–20K) · Conquest Flamebar ($15K Full Executive, Jun 2026).

Pick the exemplar matching the chosen tier as the base; the Notion master defines the placeholders.

## Structure (from the signed exemplars)

1. **Header** — "STATEMENT OF WORK | CONFIDENTIAL · THE AI TEAM SPRINT · A program for [CLIENT/team]."
2. **Engagement framing** — for coaching-graduate sponsors: "This engagement brings the work you and I have done together to your team."
3. **Program shape** — seven weeks: five weekly sessions bookended by a two-week Pre-Sprint and a Post-Sprint summary; cohort band; the from→to outcome sentence.
4. **The five sessions** — titles + durations (S01 2 hrs; S02–05 75 min; note any custom build session).
5. **Tier deliverables** — exactly the chosen tier ($10,000 / $12,500 / $15,000). Middle must read as what was verbally scoped — nothing new, nothing missing.
6. **Logistics** — virtual; recordings + retention; the protected weekly window; team-lead responsibilities.
7. **Curriculum adaptation clause** — "The curriculum may be adjusted as AI capabilities evolve. Session order may shift."
8. **Investment & payment** — locked price matching the call; installments if agreed (thirds worked); ACH-upfront $1,000 discount if used; card-fee pass-through OK.
9. **Signatures** — e-signature with PDF + print/scan fallback offered proactively.

## Customization rules

- **Scope-guard:** anything beyond the tier (governance committee membership, seats beyond the cohort band, org-level consulting) gets named out-of-scope or priced as an add-on. ConForms grew 15→20 unrenegotiated and governance crept toward committee work — never repeat silently.
- Cohort band 6–15, default 10. Below 6 peer energy collapses; above 15 focus dies.
- Date math: kickoff ~1–2 weeks post-signature; two-week Pre-Sprint; sessions every 7–10 days; name travel-week gaps explicitly.
