# The Friday Email to Sneh — Format and Rules

This email has history: Danoosh has been sending "5 Content Angles to Play With, Week of ..." emails since June 2026. Sneh knows the format. Keep its bones; the update is the harder bar (3–5, not always 5), the confidence reads, and the routing line.

## Mechanics

- **Create as a Gmail draft. Never send.** Danoosh reviews and sends. (Tool: create_draft, to: sneh@scalingradcontent.com.)
- **Subject:** `N Content Angles to Play With, Week of [Month D]` — N is the honest count (3–5).
- **Timing:** Friday, or on request.
- **If material already went to Sneh mid-week** (a raw-material email, a draft), open with one housekeeping line pointing to it so nothing double-ships.

## Structure

```
Hey Sneh,

Friday. The week's loot is sorted and the good stuff is on the table.

[Optional 1-line housekeeping: what's already in her hands this week, so
nothing double-ships.]

[Standing clearance reminder whenever relevant: nothing traceable to
Pleasant Lake Partners or anyone connected to them. Contractual.]

The line: [The one sentence an executive carries out of the room. If this

The read: [A full working paragraph, 6-9 sentences: the story arc, 1-2
verbatim quotes, the turn, the landing. Enough meat that Sneh can write
from it without a follow-up call. Reader is the protagonist; composite or

Why now: [1-2 sentences. ICP fit, which theme it advances, any timing.]

Raw material: [ALWAYS. Fathom links with [MM:SS] timestamps AND the Notion

whose OK, or "composite, no approval needed, confirm before ship."]

---

Bench (with links, so nothing is lost): 1-2 lines each on what didn't
clear the bar and why, so Sneh sees the judgment, not just the output.

Warm one-line close. Sign-off: Danoosh, C: (415) 680-5730.
```

## The bar for inclusion

Each angle must pass all three filters (ICP, brand enemy, editorial test) AND have real raw material behind it: a verbatim quote, a lived moment, a specific artifact. No "AI is changing leadership"-shaped abstractions. If the week yields fewer than three genuine angles, say so in the draft and flag it to Danoosh; do not lower the bar to hit the count. That has never happened in a week with client calls; the usual problem is surplus, which the ranking and the routing solve.

## Pre-draft checklist

- [ ] Every angle leads with "The line" and passes sharp-AND-simple (low cognitive load for a busy executive)
- [ ] Taste Gate run on every angle: share trigger named (which of the five), Onlyness passed (what only Danoosh has); Gate 2 failures on the bench with a one-line why
- [ ] Reader-as-protagonist check: no angle is framed as "what my client did wrong"
- [ ] Source-will-read-it check: composite for rooms, tribute register for individuals; would each source feel honored?
- [ ] Pleasant Lake Partners scrub: nothing traceable, including indirect tells (industry, role, recognizable details)
- [ ] Aspiration-vs-practice verified for every client story (check the transcript, not the summary)
- [ ] Anonymization + aspirational glossing applied ("a CEO I work with", no industry anchor unless it IS the story); NEEDS APPROVAL line on every client-derived angle
- [ ] Gender check: composites and hypotheticals include women and men; no male-default stereotyping
- [ ] Each angle has exactly one live wire, quoted verbatim where possible
- [ ] Links on EVERY angle and every bench item: Fathom with timestamps + Notion cards
- [ ] LEAD/TEST assigned honestly (a week of five LEADs means the bar slipped)
- [ ] Routing assigned; anything flagship-grade is held for newsletter or book, and says so
- [ ] Voice check: reads like Danoosh talking to a trusted collaborator, no em dashes, no hype, talking WITH never down or at
